100 most traveled airline city pairs, United States

July 2025 to June 2026

Passenger origin–destination journeys between city markets, both directions combined, including trips with stops.

    A roundtrip journey counts as two journeys (one each way). Average daily journeys each way are total journeys over the 12-month period divided by 365 divided by 2. “With stops” are trips with a change of flight (two or more flight coupons).

    Methodology & sources

    What is counted

    • Each BTS DB1C Market record is one passenger origin–destination journey within BTS market boundaries. A New York–Atlanta–Orlando connection counts only in the New York–Orlando market.
    • Both directions are combined into one pair, and a round trip normally counts once in each direction. These are journeys, not unique people. Average daily journeys each way = 12-month journeys ÷ 365 ÷ 2.
    • Trips that start and end in the same city market are excluded, as are a few rail code-share points with no BTS airport ID (e.g., New York Penn Station).

    Sample and period

    • DB1C is a 40% ticket sample. Each sampled record is multiplied by 2.5.
    • The 12 monthly files for reporting months July 2025–June 2026 are summed. Nothing is annualized.

    “With stops”

    • Trips with a change of flight: markets with two or more flight coupons. Trips on one coupon count as no stops, including the small share of through flights with an intermediate stop on the same flight (about 0.3–0.4%).
    • The percentage is computed from the pooled 12-month totals.

    City markets

    • Airports are grouped using BTS city markets throughout, with no metro-area adjustments. Examples: New York City = JFK, EWR, LGA, HPN, ISP, SWF; Los Angeles = LAX, SNA, BUR, ONT, LGB; Chicago = ORD, MDW; Washington - Baltimore = BWI, DCA, IAD; Miami - Fort Lauderdale = MIA, FLL. West Palm Beach, Orlando Sanford (SFB), and Paine Field (PAE) are separate BTS markets.
    • City market 1 is the market with more total air journeys across all pairs. The order does not affect the counts.
    • Domestic includes Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories. Trips on international tickets, including their U.S. flights, are not counted.
